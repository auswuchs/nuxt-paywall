# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: a parent who decides whether to pay for a kids' game. Secondary: the child holding the phone when the paywall appears inside the game's webview. The child should want what's behind the wall; the parent should understand exactly what they pay and feel safe doing it.

## Product Purpose

A mobile paywall that unlocks full content of a conditional kids' game. Success: the parent picks a plan and completes the (test) purchase without confusion, and sees a clear confirmation.

## Positioning

All levels unlocked, no ads, no surprise charges: a fair deal a parent can say yes to in one glance.

## Brand Personality

Bold, cheerful, confident, in the spirit of Duolingo: flat saturated colors, chunky tactile buttons, short friendly copy. Playful at the top for the child, calm and plain around prices for the parent.

## Anti-references

Dark-pattern paywalls: hidden prices, fake countdown timers, tiny close buttons, guilt copy. Generic SaaS pricing tables.

## Design Principles

- Price is never hidden or ambiguous: what you pay and how often is readable at a glance.
- Fun on top, clarity below: delight lives in the hero, not in the plan cards.
- Big, obvious touch targets for small and impatient fingers.
- One primary action per screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text contrast at least WCAG AA. Touch targets at least 44×44. Selected plan is distinguished by more than color alone. Respect reduced motion.
